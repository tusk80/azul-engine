How the puzzles are made

The engine's neural network played 200 games against itself. At every turn it searched the position with two candidate lines. A position became a puzzle when three things held: the search reached the end of the round, so nothing was cut short; the best move scored at least 3 points more than the second best; and the best move was not the one a greedy player would pick, the move that looks best for your own board right now.

That last rule is what makes them puzzles. The answer is often a quiet move: leaving a colour for the opponent so they have to break a line, taking the first-player token at the right moment, or dropping tiles on the floor now to avoid a bigger penalty later. The code is azul puzzles in the source on GitHub.
