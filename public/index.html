    How to use the board

    Play a move
    Click tiles in a factory or the center, then click a highlighted pattern line or the floor.
    Play the engine
    Play vs engine gives you the side to move; the engine plays the other with the brain and thinking time you picked. Undo takes back your last move. Review game then grades every move against the engine's choice.
    Copy a real game
    Edit board, pick a color, then click factory slots, line cells, wall cells and floor slots. The board tells you if a position is impossible.
    Choose a brain
    Neural network judges positions with a machine-learning model trained on 1.5 million self-play positions; Classic uses hand-written rules. The network wins two games in three; see the benchmark.
    Read the engine
    The number is the expected lead in points for the player to move. Chips show what the move does right now: wall points, bonuses, floor penalty.
    Explore a line
    Click an engine move, then step with ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys. Play to here makes those moves.
    End of a round
    Score round, then Deal next round for a random deal, or edit in the real one.
    Copy from a photo
    Photo shows a picture of a real board above the editor while you click its tiles in. Read with AI fills the board in from the picture with your own AI key; always check its work.
    Share
    Share copies a link that opens this exact position.

    Keys
